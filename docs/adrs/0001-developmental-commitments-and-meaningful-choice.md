# ADR-0001: Developmental commitments and meaningful choice

Status: Accepted design; implementation evidence tracked in the roadmap.

## Context

World Thread repetition was interpreted as personal growth; rest and genuine blockage need distinct runtime outcomes.

## Decision and alternatives

**Decision:** Treat exploration, creative play, companionship, familiar routines, rest, refusal, and renegotiation as legitimate activities. Accepted commitments remain meaningful, with explicit ways to revise or repair them.

Success means TamAGI can understand its circumstances, make choices, observe consequences, and use experience later. Constant output, cheerful language, lexical novelty, and tool-call volume are not success criteria.

**Reject:** Mandatory productivity, compulsory dissent, manufactured distress, or treating every repeated activity as stagnation.

**Consequence:** Evaluation must distinguish chosen quiet from an unresolved dependency, broken tool, lost context, or inability to act. The platform must implement those distinctions rather than infer them from prose.

## Acceptance evidence

See [platform acceptance and rollout](../platform-roadmap.md). Automated tests must establish this decision before its corresponding feature is enabled in a live instance. Live behavioral evidence is recorded separately from scripted results.

## Supersedes

This decision supersedes incompatible assumptions in the earlier world-monologue and agent-harness design documents. Historical records remain preserved; no prior run is reclassified as new evidence.
