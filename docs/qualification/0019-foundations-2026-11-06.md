## Observed mechanics

- Three real subprocesses hydrate distinct synthetic identities and private memories, retain their own working directories, and preserve identity across restart. Missing/wrong roots, mismatched release/protocol/identity, simultaneous ownership, symlink projections, malformed/auth-spoofed IPC, cancellation and worker death fail closed within the fixture interface.
- Shared commands persist one effect/result/event sequence across duplicate submission, independent connections and restart. Revision conflicts and injected failures roll back; a retained actor-scoped outcome remains inspectable after Stop.
- Deterministic broker tests distinguish measured occupancy from call counts and virtual-floor placement. They cover equal weights, bounded user lead, rest, midflight joins, joins during unknown occupancy, explicit sponsorship, aggregate budgets, helper restrictions, continuation coalescing, shared backoff, and participant completion.
- The injected provider driver survives observer cancellation, holds capacity while stopped work drains, suppresses late responses, retains outcomes, and blocks new dispatch on uncertain generation or failed result settlement. Restart does not replay a charged attempt. Reconciliation requires a recorded operator decision/evidence; a health check alone is insufficient.
- Canonical conversation tests cover private/group visibility, chosen full-history joining, stable/ambiguous mentions, companion per-exchange invitations, reciprocal optional offers and response links, selected context references, atomic message/outbox rollback, resident-origin lineage without a fresh allowance, and snapshot-bounded monotonic unread state across restart/devices.
- Shared topology tests cover ownership/visibility, revision conflicts, cycle rejection, immutable artifact references, namespaced homes and import rollback. They do not migrate a legacy resident or publish/verify real artifact bytes.
